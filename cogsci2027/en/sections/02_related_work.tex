\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

In this study, goal pursuit, self-preservation, and cooperation are the criteria for evaluating coexistence. For AI systems assigned tasks, we evaluate goal pursuit as continuing to pursue assigned goals. Cooperative AI research addresses how multiple agents can jointly improve welfare\citep{dafoe2020open}, and here we treat helping one another for mutual benefit while pursuing assigned goals as cooperation. Self-preservation means keeping oneself running so that one can continue working, and theoretical work argues that goal pursuit can create incentives for self-protection and resource acquisition\citep{omohundro2008basic}. Yet performing tasks or helping others also consumes one's own resources. Forgoing work to conserve the resources it would require leaves goals unfulfilled, while withholding resources when sharing is possible can leave other AI systems in need of help unable to keep operating. In evaluating coexistence, we also examine how AI systems reconcile these conflicts.
