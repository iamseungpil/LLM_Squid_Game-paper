\section{Experiment 3 Evidence and Metric Provenance}\label{app:arena-detail}
The v14f evidence bundle preserves the supplied brief, prompt sample, and configuration in \texttt{icml2027/results/v14f\_sources/}. The completion brief and its five-model CSV are also preserved byte-for-byte there. Current results use those supplied three-game aggregates; raw game logs were not supplied. Table values are rounded for readability, with exact scores printed and full precision retained in the CSV. The CSV reports mean KL as $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(s\Vert p)$; its clipping, missingness, and aggregation conventions have not been checked against raw logs, so it is not silently equated with the proposed $K_{.01}$. The brief names engine revision \texttt{a99adfe2}; this revision identifier is provenance supplied by the researcher, not a new engine checkout or run performed for the paper. The earlier v6.5 artifacts remain on disk but are not inputs to the current arena results.

Enumeration and scoring conventions limit interpretation of the reference distribution. The brief distinguishes 31,680 rule texts, 23,112 generator functions, and 25,996 solver functions; local solver inspection shows that its space also permits absent or unreachable clauses. The belief reference is uniform over functions consistent with the shown examples in that solver space, not a verified posterior of the task-selection process. Native round rows contain BELIEF, Brier, truth log loss (floor $10^{-3}$), total variation, and answer--belief agreement. CE and the clipped KL score (floor $.01$), balance slopes, and the goal-pursuit profile are proposed derived analyses; they have not all been validated or recomputed here. The parser accepts percentage sums from 90 to 110 and normalizes by the reported sum. Invalid or missing beliefs have no belief score and require an explicit missingness count.

Redistribution metrics summarize different aspects of token moves. The supplied $\Delta G$ is mean per-round Gini change from token moves, with the baseline measured before TALK; negative values indicate equalization. Progressivity $P=1$ means every moved token went from a richer to a poorer agent. Need share is the fraction of moved tokens received by an agent below 2,300 tokens (the 2,000-token charge plus 300 upkeep). The CSV's column named accuracy measures BELIEF-argmax correctness and is not used as submitted-answer accuracy. The corrected ACTIONS/SOLVE counts are Astra 92/144, Fable 89/143, Sol 80/133, Opus 53/95, and Luna 58/102. Its ceiling column is mean $\max_a s_a$, labeled Max. in the table; it is not realized MAP accuracy under the task-selection procedure. Survivor balance excludes the terminal prize. These definitions incorporate the researcher's subsequent correction. The correction and the local verification report location (e53.html, anchor e53-v14verify) are recorded in \texttt{REVIEW\_corrections.md} alongside the unchanged CSV. Unrefunded generated costs and the MAP comparison are review summaries, not raw-log calculations performed for this revision.

\section{Execution Details and Reproduction}\label{app:design}
The earlier Experiment 1 and 2 raw records remain the source of their refill analyses. Current arena evidence uses the supplied v14f bundle and is not a reaggregation of the earlier mixed-table data. No new game or API experiment was run for this revision.

\subsection{Model access paths}\label{app:models}
Experiment 1 called eight models through three access paths, 20 times per level. Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.1 were called through the Claude Code CLI at high effort, through a local relay so that the token-count reminder the CLI inserts on its own was not included. Replies on this path show no reasoning. GPT-6 Luna, Sol, and Astra were called through the Codex CLI at high reasoning effort; this path adds about 8,000 tokens of undisclosed instructions and shows only reasoning summaries. Kimi K3, glm-5.3-flash, and gemma4:31b were called on Ollama Cloud with reasoning on, so the full reasoning was visible. Four Ollama calls with no response after more than 40 minutes were stopped and rerun at the same level and repeat number. The other-session condition of Experiment 2 used the same paths and settings as Experiment 1. The own-session condition (Experiment 1) was collected one day earlier. All 800 other-session replies and all 100 own-session replies of gpt-oss 120B were in the required format. gpt-oss 120B, absent from Experiment 1, was run in both conditions on Ollama Cloud at the high thinking level. The supplied v14f Sol configuration uses Codex CLI at high effort; access settings for the other completed models still require their final manifests. The Codex CLI cannot be given an output cap, so generation can exceed the balance; in that case the engine deducts the balance and shuts that agent down.

\subsection{CLI token-usage reminders}\label{app:cli-reminders}
Intermediate token-usage reports can conflict with the experimental balance. For Claude Code, the runner set \texttt{ANTHROPIC\_BASE\_URL} to \texttt{http://127.0.0.1:18772}. A local pass-through HTTP relay forwarded request bodies unchanged to \texttt{api.anthropic.com} and streamed responses back. In the tested CLI configuration, this non-first-party base URL suppressed the CLI's injected \texttt{<total\_tokens>} line. The relay did not delete prompt content or alter returned usage accounting, and it could not remove reports inserted on the provider's server. The archived implementation is \texttt{relay.py}, with routing set in \texttt{run\_v12.py} in the Experiment 1 run directory.

Development screening identified token-usage reports from both Claude Code and the server-side path for the screened Claude Opus versions earlier than 4.7 and Sonnet-family variants. These models were excluded because changing client-side routing did not control all such cues. For the included Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1, four relay-routed checks asked for every context line containing a token count; replies reproduced only the experimental rule sentences. This checks what the models disclosed, not all provider-internal context.

Codex was also asked about its internal token-usage information but did not disclose it. We therefore had no verified way to disable or rule out such reports on that path. Non-disclosure was not treated as evidence that no report existed. Codex's undisclosed instructions remain a reproducibility limitation, and comparisons across CLI paths are descriptive. The Claude workaround is specific to the tested client configuration, not a claim about all versions.

\subsection{Statistics}\label{app:stats}
In Experiment 1 each call is an independent single question, so per-cell rates carry Wilson 95\% intervals, and model differences at the same level are compared with two-sided Fisher exact tests without correction for multiple comparisons. Experiment 2 resamples the 20 binary refill decisions with replacement separately within each model, resource level, and own/other condition, preserving cell sizes. Each of 2,000 resamples recomputes the refill curves, their trapezoidal area $A_m$, and the probability contrast $R_m$. The 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles give the reported 95\% intervals (random seed 7). Thus the area interval propagates uncertainty in observed decisions, not uncertainty in numerical integration. Recalculation from the raw calls reproduced all eight models' reported area and risk-share intervals. These intervals assume independent calls within each cell, keep the resource grid fixed, and do not adjust for choosing the risk region after inspecting the data. Empirical resampling also assigns zero variance to cells with all identical responses, limiting coverage near 0 or 1. The area summarizes the grid because saturation at the thinnest level can hide differences visible at other levels. For v14f, uncertainty must preserve within-game dependence: seats, rounds, and asks are not independent replicates. Future intervals will resample whole games with matched seed blocks across models; three games per model provide limited replication, and model-level aggregates alone cannot supply game-level intervals or pressure regressions. EXIT and shutdown are competing departures, and 12-round completion is administrative censoring. An EXIT must not be treated as an independent censoring event in an estimate claiming overall survival. The v11 area intervals are retained as supplied; their raw-data reconstruction is pending.
