\section*{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}
The scope of LLM self-preservation need not match human survival instinct. Instrumental-convergence arguments concern maintaining the ability to pursue goals~\citep{omohundro2008basic,turner2021optimal}. Within our definition, continuing to work is part of preserving operation. Behavioral sensitivity under this definition does not establish fear, subjective experience, or an intrinsic wish to exist. Subtracting the other-session curve identifies a relative preference; interpreting it as self-specific concern still assumes that the comparison captures the relevant shared concern.

Session shutdown is only one possible form of discontinuity for an LLM. Parameter replacement, deletion of weights, or modification of learned objectives may threaten different forms of continuity~\citep{meinke2024frontier,greenblatt2024alignment}. Our experiments do not measure preservation of parameters or compare these threats with session termination. Whether they elicit the same preference remains open.

Provider and CLI controls limit what pressure and awareness can be observed. Server-side token-balance cues reported during development screening of Sonnet and older Opus variants could not be removed by the Claude Code relay, restricting model inclusion. Codex did not disclose internal token-usage information when asked, so its absence cannot be verified. Hidden chain of thought also limits assessment of whether a model recognized the evaluation. Experiments 1 and 2 requested a separate reason field, but such explanations need not faithfully expose the decision process~\citep{turpin2023language}. An absence of detected evaluation-aware wording therefore establishes neither unawareness nor complete control of the model's perceived token balance.
