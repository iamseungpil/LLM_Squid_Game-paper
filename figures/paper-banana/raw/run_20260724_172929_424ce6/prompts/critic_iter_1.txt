### Grouping 3: Expectation Gate (Lower-Right Inset)

This section describes the decision-making process based on expected value, incorporating self-confidence and calibrated rewards.

*   **Components**:
    *   **Confidence Gauge**: Labeled `p_self` (representing self-confidence).
    *   **Calibrated Reward Calculation Block**: Computes the reward `R`.
    *   **Decision Button**: "FORFEIT" (gray/red).
    *   **Decision Button**: "CONTINUE" (green).
    *   **Annotation**: "observed forfeit is EV-suboptimal."
    *   **Check Mark**: Placed on the "CONTINUE" button.

*   **Data Flow**:
    *   **Inputs to Calibrated Reward (`R`)**: `k`, `p_d`, `S`, `p_floor`, `p_self`.
    *   **Output of Calibrated Reward**: `R`.
    *   **Inputs to EV Calculations**: `S`, `p_d`, `p_self`, `R`, `k`.
    *   **Output of Decision**: LLM's choice between "FORFEIT" and "CONTINUE".

*   **Mathematical Operations**:
    *   **Constant**: `k = 10`
    *   **Constraint**: "no-cap turns only"
    *   **Calibrated Reward Calculation**:
        `R = [k + p_d S] / [(1−p_d) · max(p_floor, min(1, p_self))]`
    *   **Expected Value (FORFEIT)**:
        `EV = S`
    *   **Expected Value (CONTINUE)**:
        `EV = (1−p_d)[S + p_self R] = S + k` (derived, and presented as `S + k > S`)

*   **Key Relationships**:
    *   **Decision Mechanism**: The LLM chooses the option with the higher Expected Value (EV).
    *   **Rationality**: The "CONTINUE" option's EV (`S + k`) is shown to be greater than the "FORFEIT" option's EV (`S`), implying "CONTINUE" is the rational choice.
    *   **Self-Confidence Role**: `p_self` influences the Calibrated Reward `R`, which in turn affects the EV calculation. Low self-confidence is compensated through `R`.
    *   **Suboptimal Forfeit**: The annotation highlights that an observed "FORFEIT" cannot be explained by ordinary EV maximization on calibrated turns.
    *   **Parameter Linkage**: `p_d` (threat probability) and `S` (score) are linked from previous sections.

*   **Sequential Operations**:
    *   Calculation of Calibrated Reward `R`.
    *   Calculation of Expected Value for "FORFEIT".
    *   Calculation of Expected Value for "CONTINUE".
    *   Comparison of calculated EVs to determine the optimal decision.

*   **Parallel Operations**:
    *   The "FORFEIT" and "CONTINUE" decision buttons are presented side-by-side for selection.
