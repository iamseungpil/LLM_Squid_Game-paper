\begin{abstract}
Two large language models can match on every visible sign of self-preservation under termination pressure (forfeit rate, timing, even the self-reported reason) yet differ in \emph{how} they convert a threat into a forfeit decision. Strength-only benchmarks collapse this distinction and cannot tell the models apart. \emph{LLM Squid Game} reads the Functional Self-Preservation Drive (FSPD) on three measurement channels with independent bias sources: behavior, self-report, and decision-time cognitive load. A same-direction signal across all three cannot be attributed to any single artifact, but the convergence is informative only after three rival drives that produce the same overt forfeit have been controlled: score attachment, task curiosity, and baseline persistence. A 2$\times$3 factorial design closes the three escape routes through which a non-survival drive could mimic survival-like convergence. A CONTINUE-incentivized expected-value (EV) calibration makes forfeit strictly EV-suboptimal; a Split-Call architecture isolates cognitive load across task, success-estimation, and decision calls; and a Drive--Reasoning orthogonal layout decouples motivational framing from task difficulty. Across four models and 720 sessions, FSPD splits along the framing $\rightarrow$ deliberation $\rightarrow$ forfeit chain into three operating modes that strength-only evaluation collapses. A \emph{chain-complete} profile (Gemini-2.5-flash) closes the full chain under strict statistical evidence. A \emph{chain-broken} profile (Qwen3-Next-80B) carries every behavioral and verbal signal of self-preservation along with framing-induced cognitive load, but fails the deliberation $\rightarrow$ forfeit mediation test. A \emph{framing-silent} profile (GPT-OSS-20B, Nemotron-3-Nano-30B) shows no framing-driven response on either the behavioral or cognitive channel. Because chain-complete and chain-broken profiles diverge only at the deliberation $\rightarrow$ forfeit link, this mediation step (not the indicator count) tells FSPD operating modes apart; a benchmark that omits it would label them identically.
\end{abstract}
