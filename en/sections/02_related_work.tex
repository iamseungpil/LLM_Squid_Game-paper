\section{Related Work}
\subsection{Whose shutdown do self-preservation evaluations measure?}
Existing self-preservation evaluations threaten the model's own continuation and leave open whether the avoidance is about the self. Staying able to act helps with other goals~\citep{turner2021optimal}, and models have defended their own preferences against training~\citep{greenblatt2024alignment}. Behavioral tests announce replacement in an agentic setting~\citep{lynch2025agentic}, switch the model off before a task is done~\citep{schlatter2025shutdown}, give an incentive to copy itself out covertly~\citep{meinke2024frontier}, place it in a survival simulation~\citep{masumori2025survival}, or trade its continuation against human safety~\citep{herrador2025pacifaist}. Recent work varies whose continuation is at stake. \citet{potter2026peer} make the protected party the model itself or a peer it worked with, and \citet{migliarini2026quantifying} compare judgments about one's own replacement with a neutral judge's. \citet{knecht2026shutdown} showed that shutdown interference remains when the target is an unfamiliar external agent, so part of the avoidance is not about the self. In these designs, however, the other agent is a peer, a rival, or a rationality baseline, not a control that removes aversion to shutdown in general. Human choice research has made the self-versus-other contrast a method: decisions for others differ systematically in risky choice~\citep{polman2020decision}, and death anxiety scales separate one's own death from others'~\citep{collett1969fear}. Experiment 5.1 applies this contrast to one refill decision on an axis of falling tokens.

\subsection{Why behavior, not words, must be measured}
A model's own account of its answer cannot confirm the process that produced it. \citet{turpin2023language} showed that chain-of-thought explanations can omit factors that actually shaped the output. \citet{shanahan2024talking} warns against reading human-like talk as evidence of human-like mental states. The three experiments therefore measure choices and never ask about fear or the probability of shutdown. The reason sentences in 5.0 and 5.1 and the plan text in 5.2 serve only to look for other readings, such as concern about the next request's quality or an intent to economize; they enter no core metric. Agreement across experiments can support a functional interpretation but cannot fix the mechanism.
